# Set up your barcode scanner

This page helps you set up the scanner that came in your box and fix common problems. You don't need to sign in to read it.

## What you need

- The scanner, its dock, the USB-C cable, and the wall power adapter from the box.
- The invite link, which you get separately by email or text. It is not in the box.
- Your phone.
- Your home Wi-Fi name and password.
- A printer. If you don't have one, you can scan the barcode from your phone or computer screen instead.

Setup takes about 15 minutes.

## Know your scanner's screen

The screen has three parts:

- **Top:** the mode. Amber means **STOCK OUT** (taking things off the shelf). Teal means **STOCK IN** (putting groceries away).
- **Middle:** what you scanned.
- **Bottom:** what happened.

The scanner has a trigger for scanning and a separate mode button.

## Set up the scanner

### 1. Make your account

1. Open the invite link you were sent.
2. Make your account and name your household.

You'll see your new, empty household in the app.

[SCREENSHOT PENDING #453]

### 2. Print the setup sheet

1. In the app, open **Menu (☰) → Household → Scanners**.
2. Tap **Connect a scanner**.
3. Tap **Print setup & wall sheet**. Print at Actual size (100%), not Fit to page.

You'll see one page with a pairing barcode on top and a wall card below a dashed line.

The pairing barcode works once. The sheet shows the time it stops working, 10 minutes after you open **Connect a scanner**. Do steps 3 and 4 before then. If you run out of time, get a new one in the app and print again.

No printer? Leave the **Connect a scanner** window open. In step 4, scan the barcode right off your phone or computer screen.

### 3. Connect the scanner to Wi-Fi

1. Put the scanner on its dock and plug the dock into the wall.
2. Check that the screen says `READY TO SET UP`, `Join Wi-Fi:`, and a network name like `Scanner-Setup-XXXX`.
3. On your phone, join that Wi-Fi network. A page called `Scanner Setup` opens.
4. Type your home Wi-Fi name and password, then tap **Save**. The page says `Connecting to your Wi-Fi...`.

You'll see `CONNECT HOUSEHOLD` and `Scan the pairing barcode to connect` on the scanner.

[PHOTO PENDING #453]

### 4. Connect the scanner to your household

1. Hold the scanner 10 to 30 cm (4 to 12 in) from the pairing barcode.
2. Pull the trigger.

You'll hear a chime. The screen says `CONNECTED`, your household's name, and `Ready to scan`. In the app, the **Connect a scanner** window closes and the scanner shows **Online**.

[PHOTO PENDING #453]

### 5. Try it, then choose how it works

1. Scan two things from your pantry.
2. Look at the screen after each scan.
   - The first time you scan something, the screen says `NEW BARCODE`, `Unknown item`, and `In Scan Queue to name`. That's expected. Open the Scan Queue in the app and give each item a name.
   - Once an item has a name, the screen shows `STOCK OUT`, the item's name, and something like `Queued -1 - 3 on hand`.
3. In the app, the scans wait in the Scan Queue until you confirm them.
4. On the scanner's card in **Menu (☰) → Household → Scanners**, find **When an item is scanned** and pick one:
   - **Require review in Scan Queue:** nothing changes until you confirm. Good with kids around.
   - **Adjust stock immediately for known items:** known items update right away. New barcodes still go to the Scan Queue.
5. Cut out the wall card along the dashed line and tape it next to the scanner.

The scanner starts in Stock out. After you switch to Stock in, it goes back to Stock out 5 minutes after your last scan. The bottom of the screen counts down, for example `Back to Stock out: 4:59`. To change the time, use **Return to default mode after** on the scanner's card.

## If something looks wrong

Find what your scanner's screen says, then follow the steps.

### `OFFLINE` in the top corner

Keep scanning. The scanner saves up to 500 scans and sends them when Wi-Fi is back. If it stays `OFFLINE` after your Wi-Fi is working, see [Your Wi-Fi changed](#your-wi-fi-changed).

### `Saved offline` and `{N} waiting to send`

These scans are saved on the scanner. They send on their own when the scanner is back on Wi-Fi. Don't disconnect or erase the scanner while scans are waiting, or they are lost.

### `MEMORY FULL` or `NOT SAVED`

The scanner is holding 500 scans and won't take new ones. Get it back on Wi-Fi. The saved scans then send, and you can scan again. Scan again any item that showed `NOT SAVED`.

### `SCANNER ALERT`, `Disconnected`, `from household`

Someone disconnected this scanner from your household. Scans it hadn't sent are lost.

1. Print a new setup sheet: **Menu (☰) → Household → Scanners → Connect a scanner → Print setup & wall sheet**.
2. Scan the new pairing barcode.
3. Scan again the items whose scans were lost.

### `PAIRING CODE EXPIRED` or `CODE NOT ACCEPTED`

That pairing barcode is too old, was already used, or didn't work. Print a new setup sheet and scan it.

### `TOO MANY TRIES`

Wait a minute, then scan the setup sheet again.

### `CAN'T CONNECT`

Check that your home Wi-Fi is working, then scan the setup sheet again. If your Wi-Fi name or password changed, see [Your Wi-Fi changed](#your-wi-fi-changed).

### `NEW BARCODE`, `Unknown item`, `In Scan Queue to name`

This item isn't in your list yet. Open the Scan Queue in the app and give it a name. Next time, the scanner knows it.

### The mode is wrong

The screen says `STOCK IN` when you're taking things out, or the reverse. Scan the matching barcode on your wall card, or press the mode button. If the screen is dark, the first press only wakes it.

### `OFF DOCK`, `Put me back on the dock to charge`

Put the scanner back on its dock.

### Your Wi-Fi changed

Use this after a new router or a new Wi-Fi password. The scanner stays connected to your household and keeps its saved scans.

1. Hold the mode button until the screen says `HOLDING` and `Let go now to update Wi-Fi` (about 5 seconds).
2. Let go. The screen says `WI-FI SETUP` and shows a network name like `Scanner-Setup-XXXX`.
3. On your phone, join that network. On the `Scanner Setup` page, type your Wi-Fi name and new password, then tap **Save**.

Let go while the screen says `HOLDING`. If you hold until the screen turns red and says `LET GO TO ERASE`, letting go erases the scanner.

### Start over (erases the scanner)

Do this only when nothing else works. It erases the scanner's Wi-Fi, its connection to your household, and any scans it hasn't sent.

1. Hold the mode button until the screen turns red and says `LET GO TO ERASE` (about 10 seconds).
2. Let go. The screen says `READY TO SET UP`.
3. Do steps 2, 3, and 4 again with a new setup sheet.

## Printing tips

- Print at Actual size (100%), not Fit to page.
- The setup sheet has a line that should measure 50 mm (about 2 inches). If it's shorter, print again at 100%.
- Black and white is fine. Every color on the sheet also has a word next to it.
